# 01 · Customer brief: Copperleaf Lab Supplies

> **FICTIONAL CUSTOMER.** Copperleaf Lab Supplies Pvt. Ltd., its people and its numbers are invented for this portfolio. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

**Summary.** A 60-person lab equipment distributor in Kolkata and Bengaluru is moving payroll from Excel and an external CA to an HR and payroll platform. October 2026 salaries must be paid from the platform on Friday 30 October. The main risks are late data from the outgoing CA, a salary structure that has not been reviewed against the Labour Codes, and a Durga Puja office closure in the middle of the payroll window.

## Company

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | One private limited company. One PAN and TAN. |
| Locations | Kolkata HQ and warehouse (West Bengal): 44 employees. Bengaluru sales and service office (Karnataka): 16 employees. |
| Registrations | PF and ESI (one code each). Professional Tax and Labour Welfare Fund in both states. Portal logins are held by the CA. |
| Out of scope (to confirm) | 4 consultants paid on invoice, not through payroll. |

## Pay structures

| Group | Headcount | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Staff (managers, sales, engineers, finance) | 46 | Monthly CTC = basic + HRA + special allowance + employer PF. Employer PF capped at 12% of ₹15,000. Basic is 40% of CTC for some longer-serving staff and 50% for the rest. |
| Warehouse and office support (Kolkata) | 14 | Gross at or below ₹21,000, so ESI applies. Same components. |
| Bengaluru sales incentive | 12 of the staff above | Quarterly incentive, calculated by the sales head in a separate sheet. |
| PF opt-outs | 2 senior staff | Not PF members. The CA has not documented why. |

## How payroll runs today

The office admin tracks attendance and leave in Excel and emails it to the CA around the 22nd. The CA returns a salary register and bank file. The finance lead uploads the file to the bank. Payslips go out as emailed PDFs.

## Current problems

1. Three of the last six payrolls needed corrections, mostly loss-of-pay disputes caused by late leave data.
2. In February 2026 Karnataka PT was deducted at ₹200 instead of ₹300 and corrected late.
3. Bank detail changes arrive by email and are not verified.
4. Full-and-final settlements take over 45 days.
5. No one has checked the 40% basic structure against the Labour Code wage definition.

## Stakeholders

| Person | Role in project | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| Founder and MD | Sponsor. Final payroll approver. | No employee complaints. No surprise costs. |
| Finance lead (title: CFO) | Owns payroll accuracy, bank release and statutory payments. | Figures that reconcile to the CA's. Sceptical of the switch. |
| Office admin | Owns attendance, leave, joiner and leaver data. | Less manual work. Limited Excel skills. |
| External CA (outgoing) | Holds history: year-to-date pay, TDS, investment declarations, portal logins. | Contract ends 31 October. Little reason to prioritise us. |

## Go-live constraints

- October 2026 salaries credited on Friday 30 October from the platform. The CA contract is not extended.
- Kolkata office closed for Durga Puja, Monday 19 to Thursday 22 October (company holiday calendar).
- West Bengal PT slabs are reported to change from 1 October 2026 [S4], so October is the first month under the new slabs.
