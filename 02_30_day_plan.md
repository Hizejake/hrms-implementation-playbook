# 30-Day Go-Live Plan for Copperleaf Lab Supplies

## Week 1-2: Data Cleanup and Parallel Runs (Sep 16 - Sep 29)

* Review and clean up employee data to ensure accuracy and completeness
* Conduct parallel runs to test system functionality and identify potential issues

## Week 3: Training and Contingency Planning (Sep 30 - Oct 6)

* Provide training to employees on new HRMS system
* Develop contingency plan for Durga Puja closure (Oct 19-22)

## Week 4: CA Handover and PT Slab Change (Oct 7 - Oct 13)

* Hand over PF/ESI/PT registrations to new CA
* Implement West Bengal PT slab change (Oct 1)

## Week 5: Final Preparations and Testing (Oct 14 - Oct 20)

* Conduct final testing and quality assurance
* Make any necessary adjustments to employee data or system configuration

## Week 6: Go-Live and Post-Implementation Review (Oct 21 - Oct 30)

* Go live with new HRMS system
* Conduct post-implementation review and identify areas for improvement
